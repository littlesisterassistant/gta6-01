SOLMAR HEAT — an open-world crime story in Solmar Bay. Jax Moreno owes Mama Lupe $5,800 and she collects in favours, not cash. Five jobs and the debt is clear — if the Solmar police let him finish them.

Explore a sun-soaked coastal city on foot and by car: steal sedans, taxis, trucks and the red Comet GT, dodge traffic and pedestrians, outrun the Solmar PD as the wanted stars climb, and work through five story missions — errands, package runs, a hot-wheels getaway, a dockside brawl and a 90-second cash-van dash. Cash, wanted heat and missions cleared are saved in your browser.

Top-down 2D canvas, vanilla JavaScript, procedural WebAudio sounds and music. No libraries, no network, no build step.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

## Controls

WASD / Arrows — walk & drive · E — enter / exit car · SHIFT — run · SPACE — handbrake · F — punch · Click — shoot (pistol) · M — minimap · P — pause · R — restart
